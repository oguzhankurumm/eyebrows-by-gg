## Platform

web

## Stack

Existing: Next.js 16 static export, Tailwind v4, shadcn-style Radix primitives, motion, react-hook-form + zod. Host: Vercel (preview only after user approval).

## Users

Women and men in New Haven County / coastal Connecticut (Milford, Orange, Stratford, West Haven, New Haven, Fairfield, Bridgeport) who want natural-looking brows or lips and fear a tattooed, drawn-on result. Often on a phone, deciding whether to book a free consultation. Includes post-chemo restoration clients and repeat clients.

## Product Purpose

Eyebrows by GG is a single-artist permanent makeup studio at 972 Boston Post Rd, Milford, CT. GG (Gonul Gonenc) is a licensed Connecticut tattoo artist with 20+ years in brow work. Services: nanoblading, powder brows, combo brows, lip blush, touch-ups, threading, henna tinting. Success = a booking on GlossGenius or a call.

## Positioning

One artist, one room, one continuous record of your brows: free 15-minute consultation, 6-week perfecting visit included, 20+ years starting from hand threading in 2005.

## Operating Context

Booking lives on GlossGenius (https://eyebrowsbygg.glossgenius.com/services). Phone +1 (203) 385-2243, info@eyebrowsbygg.com, Instagram @eyebrows_by_gg, WhatsApp. Hours Mon-Sat 10:00-20:00, Sunday closed. Promo bar: "SPECIAL OFFER $100 OFF ON PERMANENT MAKE-UP".

- Blog has near-duplicate posts; canonicalisation is a client decision.

## Brand Commitments

None binding: user declared logo, colors, and fonts replaceable. Footer credit "Website: Oguzhan Kurum" stays.

## Evidence on Hand

21 real before/after portfolio photos, service and blog images (web/public/assets/img), AI-regenerated GG portraits (need client approval). Three testimonials exist verbatim in the microblading service page. No verified review counts or client counts: must not be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Natural over dramatic: the design should feel as soft, precise and unannounced as GG's work.
2. Real work first: GG's actual results lead; no stock people.
3. One clear action: book the free consultation, always reachable on a phone.
4. Fidelity: every word of the migrated copy and SEO metadata survives.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, visible focus, reduced-motion respected, alt text on all imagery, mobile-first.
