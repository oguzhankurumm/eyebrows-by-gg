## SWOT for Eyebrows by GG

**Strengths.** The studio’s Instagram supplies an active body of real work, process videos and GG speaking about her approach. The owner-confirmed background includes 28 years in eyebrow threading, Connecticut PMU licensing and AAM Gold certification in New Jersey. Threading, tinting and PMU create several entry points for clients.

**Weaknesses.** Historic microblading and combo prices conflict. Approved review sources and some appointment lengths remain missing. The first redesign relied on generated portraits and loosely assigned treatment photographs. The legacy educational archive contains repeated topics and claims requiring source context.

**Opportunities.** Present a clear studio identity with the exact brand slogan, original Instagram work, source-linked videos, a service comparison tool, a fuller artist profile and reachable preparation/policy content. Mobile navigation can shorten the path from results to treatment to booking.

**Threats.** Nearby studios already make booking, portfolio and treatment selection easy. Generic photography and uncertain prices reduce trust. Expiring Instagram CDN links, autoplay downloads and extensive motion can harm reliability and mobile performance.

The SWOT is our inference from the public sources and supplied studio facts; it is not a measured competitive ranking.

## Implementation decisions

1. Replace the active generated portraits with verified original Instagram imagery; retain supplied files without deleting them.
2. Lead with “Let’s touch your beauty”, then explain brow/PMU/lip services and Milford plainly.
3. Add real process videos with poster frames, controls and original Instagram links. Store media locally to avoid expiring CDN dependencies.
4. Add a credibility section with the precise owner-confirmed credentials, distinguishing 28 years of threading from PMU experience.
5. Keep rounded Sora/Manrope styling. Enrich service comparison, studio information and mobile navigation without adding fake reviews, counters or guarantees.
6. Preserve all 90 old URLs and expose complete sourced website detail through progressive disclosure. Verify actual text coverage independently from visual redesign.
7. Keep the price conflicts visible in the content checklist; booking confirmation remains authoritative for current costs.

## Implemented resolution

The initial missing-price/review weaknesses are resolved: the user approved the current 28-item GlossGenius menu as the sole price/duration source, and the supplied Google Maps business yielded attributed review excerpts and current location/hours. Current treatment facts sit together; all legacy prices remain internal historical material. Mismatched active media was replaced with source-matched studio work. The user subsequently rejected public source-reference panels, so the archive is internal only.
